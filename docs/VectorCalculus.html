Chapter 2
Vector Calculus

To understand how electromagnetism works we need to have a look at how direction is defined using mathematical notation. In this chapter we will have a good tour of the field of vector calculus and learn all then necessary concepts to tackle problems.

This chapter is treated as a more condensed, and specialized chapter of M.Sc Higher Mathematics I chapter of Differential and Integral Calculus and therefore, the student is suggested to look into those chapters to get a more in depth understanding of concepts if it is required.
